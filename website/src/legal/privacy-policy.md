LosslessCut edits your files on your own computer. Your files are never uploaded anywhere, and LosslessCut has no user accounts, ads or tracking.

## Stored on your computer

Your settings, project files (`.llc`, saved next to your media files) and log files. None of this is sent anywhere.

## What the app sends over the internet

The versions from the Mac App Store and Microsoft Store don't check for news or updates. In the other versions (downloaded from GitHub or the Snap Store):

**News and updates:** when it starts, the app checks for news and for a newer version. As when you visit any website, this sends your IP address and the app version. We keep a record of these requests only as long as needed for anonymous statistics, such as how many people use each version, and never share or sell it. The update check is answered by GitHub, and [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) applies.

You can turn off the update check in Settings, or both by starting LosslessCut with `--disable-networking`.

In all versions:

- **GPS map:** if you open the GPS map of a file, map images are loaded from OpenStreetMap, which sees your IP address and the area shown.
- **Spell checking:** on Windows and Linux, the spell checker in text fields may download its dictionaries from Google, which sees your IP address.

To protect users from counterfeit copies, the store versions check whether they are genuine. Genuine copies send no information for this.

Links you click, like help pages, open in your browser and are covered by those websites' own policies.

LosslessCut is built on Electron (which includes Chromium), and that may also make requests of its own that we don't control, like the spell checker above. We list the ones we know of, and update this page when we learn of others.
